## The 23 games

### Brain

- **Eyecon**: Spot the matching symbol first. Any two cards share exactly one symbol. Tap it before Seth, a friend or the clock does.
- **Snap Match**: Memorise the cards, find the pairs. The cards flash face up, then flip. Find every pair before you run out of tries.
- **Quick Math**: True or false, against the clock. Is the sum right? Tap True or False. One slip ends the run.
- **Odd One Out**: Spot the different shade. Tap the one tile in a different shade. The grid grows and the difference shrinks.
- **Echo**: Repeat the growing sequence. Watch the pads light up, then tap them back. Every round adds one.
- **Sphinx Sudoku**: Every row, column and box, one to nine. Fill the 9x9 grid against the clock, with pencil notes. Three mistakes ends it.

### Puzzle

- **Number Stack**: Merge numbers, chain combos. Drag a tile onto an equal neighbour to merge. Chain merges for up to x5 combos.
- **Block Fit**: Fit the pieces, clear the lines. Drag pieces onto the grid. Fill a row or column to clear it.
- **Color Sort**: One colour in every tube. Lift the top ball from one tube and drop it on the same colour or an empty tube.
- **Tomb Sweeper**: Uncover the tomb, avoid the traps. Clear the tomb without waking a cobra. Your first tap is always safe.

### Arcade

- **Nova Skim**: Hop planets, skim the mines. Tap to let go of your orbit and land on the next planet. Skim mines for up to x6.
- **Stack Tower**: Drop slabs, build it high. Tap to drop the sliding slab. Overhang is cut off; a perfect drop keeps it whole.
- **Ankh Throw**: Hit the disc, never an ankh. Tap to throw ankhs into the spinning disc without touching one already stuck.
- **Brick Blitz**: Bounce the ball, smash the wall. Drag the paddle, launch, and catch power-ups: multi-ball, fire and lasers.
- **Obelisk Flap**: Tap to fly between the obelisks. Tap to flap. Every obelisk you clear scores; ankhs are a bonus.
- **Scarab Dash**: Swipe lanes, dodge the boulders. Swipe between three lanes to dodge boulders and grab ankhs.
- **Sand Slide**: Slide the tiles back into place. The classic 15-puzzle, against the clock. Plus a new board daily.

### Classics

- **Four in a Row**: Line up four before they do. Drop discs into the columns. Play Seth or a friend on one phone.
- **Dominoes**: Match the ends, empty your hand. Play a tile on a matching end or draw from the pile. Empty your hand first.
- **Senet**: The pharaohs' race game, against Seth. Throw the sticks and race five pawns off the board before Seth does.
- **Nile Seeds**: Sow the seeds, empty Seth's side. Mancala on a Nile boat. Sow seeds, earn extra turns and capture his side.
- **Kemet Draughts**: Jump, crown, and clear the board. Draughts on 8x8. Jumps are forced and kings are crowned.

### Word

- **Glyph Guess**: Find the hidden five-letter word. Six guesses to find the word, in English or Arabic. A new daily word every day.
